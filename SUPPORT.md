# Support

## Free support

- **Bugs and feature requests** — [open an issue](https://github.com/jmsansan/audiobox/issues).
  Include the audiobox version, your runtime, and a sample file or a snippet that reproduces it.
- **Questions** — use [Discussions](https://github.com/jmsansan/audiobox/discussions).

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis. There is no response-time guarantee, and
issues are prioritised by impact on the wider user base rather than by how urgent they are for one
project.

## Contact

**jose.sansan@proton.me**

For anything that doesn't belong in a public issue. Please keep bug reports and usage questions on
the tracker, where other people can find the answers.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Sponsorship

If audiobox saves you or your team time, sponsoring it is the cheapest way to keep it maintained:

- **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/jmsansan)** — recurring
- **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jmsansan)** — one-off

Sponsors at higher tiers get their logo in the README, priority triage on issues, and early input
on the roadmap.
